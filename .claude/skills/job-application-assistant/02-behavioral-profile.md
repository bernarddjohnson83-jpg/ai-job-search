# Behavioral Profile

<!-- Synthesized from self-assessment during /setup - no formal assessment (PI/DISC/Myers-Briggs) was used -->

## Overview
Bernard is a **relationship-driven, cross-functional collaborator** who blends independent ownership of his own accounts/tickets with active collaboration across support, product, and engineering. He builds long-term, trusted-advisor relationships rather than treating support as transactional, and he does his best work in a mix of structured process and self-directed initiative.

## Core Behavioral Drives

| Drive | Level | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| Autonomy / Ownership | High | Prefers owning a book of clients or tickets end-to-end rather than handing off pieces |
| Relationship-building | High | Builds recurring, trusted-advisor relationships with the same clients over time |
| Cross-functional orientation | High | Comfortable and effective working across support, product, and engineering |
| Structure vs. flexibility | Balanced | Works well within structured processes (payroll compliance, SLAs) but doesn't need heavy oversight |

## Strongest Behaviors
- **Relationship ownership:** Built a recurring portfolio of 10-15+ SMB clients who requested his support exclusively, guiding 50+ clients through payroll setup end-to-end
- **Cross-functional bridge-building:** Partners directly with engineering on bug identification/escalation and surfaces support metrics (NPS/CSAT/AHT) to inform Product prioritization
- **Process improvement:** Co-led knowledge base development that cut repeat inquiries by 20%, turning one-off fixes into scalable self-service

## How You Work Best
- A mix of independent ownership (own book of accounts/tickets) and cross-functional collaboration (with product/engineering) - not purely solo, not purely team-run
- Environments that combine structured process (payroll compliance, SLA-driven support) with room for initiative and process-improvement input
- Flexible on location: fully remote, hybrid, or onsite in Houston all work if the role itself is a strong fit

## Growth Areas (frame positively in applications)
- **Formal technical/SaaS credentials:** Strong hands-on, self-taught expertise across payroll/HR SaaS platforms; actively building formal credentials to complement it (QuickBooks Payroll Certification in progress)
- **Presenting to larger groups:** Very strong 1:1 and small-group client communication; currently developing more experience presenting to larger audiences

## Mapping to Job Posting Language

When a job posting mentions these keywords, it's a **strong behavioral fit**:
- "customer success," "onboarding," "implementation," "trusted advisor," "account management"
- "cross-functional," "voice of the customer," "collaborative culture," "SMB"
- "coaching," "mentorship," "employee input/feedback valued"

When a job posting mentions these, flag as **potential friction** (not deal-breaker):
- Strictly scripted responses with no room for judgment
- Siloed support queues with no cross-team visibility or collaboration
- High-volume ticket quotas with no ownership over specific accounts

## Management Style Preferences
- **Works well with:** Coaching-oriented managers who actively mentor and invest in growth toward the next role
- **Doesn't work well with:** Command-and-control management with no feedback loop, or cultures that dismiss employee input

## Using This in Applications
- **Cover letters:** Emphasize relationship-building, end-to-end ownership, and a coaching/growth mindset; reference wanting to work somewhere that listens to employee input
- **CV:** Emphasize cross-functional bullets (engineering collaboration, knowledge base development) alongside quantifiable client retention/expansion metrics
- **Interviews:** Lead with the SMB client portfolio and product-bug-escalation STAR examples (see `07-interview-prep.md`)
- **Don't overstate:** Not a certified engineer/developer or trained people-manager; formal SaaS/technical certifications are in progress, not completed
